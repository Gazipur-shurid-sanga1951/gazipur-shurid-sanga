Our story

Tradition with a community heart.

Since 1951, Gazipur Shurid Sanga has been a gathering place for neighbours, friends and families. Our story is shaped by shared celebrations, voluntary effort and a commitment to keeping community life vibrant.

Our beginnings

Founded in 1951, the club began its Kali Puja tradition in 1952. The annual celebration has become an important part of the club's heritage.

Our purpose

To bring people together through cultural programmes, sports, national occasions, festivals and activities that strengthen neighbourhood bonds.

What brings us together

More than a club. A part of our community.

Celebrating our shared interests and the traditions that connect neighbours across generations.

Payment checklist
